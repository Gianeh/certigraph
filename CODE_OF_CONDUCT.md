# Code of conduct

CertiGraph welcomes rigorous disagreement and expects respectful collaboration.

Participants should:

- focus criticism on ideas, code, tests, and proofs;
- assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise;
- make space for newcomers while maintaining technical standards;
- avoid harassment, insults, and discriminatory language;
- disclose conflicts of interest when relevant.

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions, and pull requests to protect the project’s quality and contributors’ safety.
